# Enterprise Mobility — Finance & Strategy Capstone

University of Washington Foster School of Business capstone project evaluating a proposed integration between Enterprise Mobility and SpotHero.

The project examines whether advance parking reservations could improve the rental experience for younger urban travelers while creating incremental revenue for Enterprise. The analysis combines strategic evaluation with a five-year NPV model, WACC benchmarking, cost and revenue assumptions, and sensitivity analysis across alternative operating scenarios.

## Financial Analysis

- Five-year NPV model for the proposed integration
- WACC benchmarking using comparable public rental-car companies
- Sensitivity analysis across adoption and commission assumptions
- Estimated implementation and recurring integration costs

## Project Context

Academic team capstone project completed at the University of Washington Foster School of Business in 2026. The financial projections are scenario-based estimates prepared for academic analysis and do not represent realized results or guidance from Enterprise Mobility or SpotHero.
